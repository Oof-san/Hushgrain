# Hushgrain artwork

The full-image hero uses landscape artwork on larger screens and a separately composed portrait on phones. See [the mobile hero brief](MOBILE-HERO-BRIEF.md) for that seventeenth photograph.

All 17 photographs were generated with the built-in imagegen tool for this café concept. They depict illustrative food and spaces. No external image host, storage proxy, stock image endpoint, or runtime image-generation service is needed.

Final assets are saved in `client/public/images/`, as optimized WebP files. The editable logo and favicon are code-native SVG. Fonts are Cormorant Garamond, DM Sans, and DM Mono, served locally through Fontsource packages.

These are the reusable final briefs. The hero was generated while the brand was being selected; its brief below uses the chosen identity.

## hero.webp

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: wide hero photograph for Hushgrain, a sophisticated independent café website. A cinematic editorial breakfast still life on a warm dark walnut café table: large ivory ceramic flat white cup with beautiful leaf latte art in the right half, a flaky golden croissant on a small ceramic plate behind it, soft linen, a tiny vase with a delicate branch. Sunlight through a window, deep espresso shadows, creamy highlights, subtle film grain, tactile food photography, sophisticated Kinfolk magazine art direction. Landscape 3:2. Close overhead three-quarter angle, composition fills the frame beautifully, dark atmospheric left half with no prominent object, focus right. No text, logos, people or watermark.
